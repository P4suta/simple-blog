# Code of Conduct

simple-blog should be a calm place to investigate hard failures and improve the
software together.

Participants are expected to be precise, respectful, and patient; critique the
work rather than the person; protect private data; and make room for people with
different experience and communication styles. Harassment, threats,
discrimination, deliberate exposure of private information, and sustained
disruptive behavior are not accepted.

This policy applies to repository discussions, issues, reviews, commits, and
other spaces where someone represents the project. The maintainer may edit or
remove contributions, lock discussions, or restrict participation when needed
to protect the community.

Use GitHub's private reporting and abuse-reporting facilities for sensitive
conduct concerns. Do not publish another person's private information as
evidence. Reports will be considered in context and handled as privately as the
available GitHub facilities allow.
